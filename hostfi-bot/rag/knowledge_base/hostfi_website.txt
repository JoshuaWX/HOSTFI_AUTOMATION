About HostFi
Name: HostFi (Host Finance)
Legal Entity: HostFi Services Ltd
Website: https://hostfi.io
Purpose: A financial platform that makes buying, selling, trading, and managing crypto easy for Nigerians.
Mission: Providing financial access for everyone (banked and unbanked) through zero-fee crypto services.

HostFi is for people in Nigeria (expanding soon) who want an easy, fast, and secure way to trade, spend, and manage their crypto. Unlike many platforms, HostFi keeps things simple and affordable with no deposit fees, free swaps, fast withdrawals, and real utility through virtual cards and free transfers.

Core Features

1. Instant Buy & Sell with Naira — Buy crypto (BTC, USDT, ETH, SOL, BNB, USDC, etc.) using naira instantly. Sell crypto and get naira in your wallet immediately. No P2P stress — direct transactions. Competitive exchange rates. Zero deposit fees.

2. Virtual Dollar Cards — Create multiple virtual cards (Mastercard or Visa). Fund with naira or USDT/USDC. Use for online payments globally (Netflix, Spotify, Amazon, shopping, subscriptions, travel bookings, etc.). Works with Apple Pay, Google Pay, Samsung Pay for contactless payments. Choose card categories (e-commerce, streaming, travel, business). Track all card transactions from a simple dashboard. Create different cards for different purposes (personal, business, subscriptions).

3. Crypto Trading & Swaps — Free crypto swaps across all supported pairs — zero fees. Instant execution. Transparent pricing — the rate shown is the rate you get. No hidden markups. Supported cryptocurrencies: BTC, USDT, ETH, SOL, BNB, DOGE, USDC, TRX, SHIB, XLM, POL, and more.

4. Multiple Wallets — Support for multiple currencies: NGN, BTC, USDT, ETH, SOL, BNB, USDC, and more. Fund wallets with fiat or crypto. Swap across currencies easily. Track everything from one dashboard.

5. Secure & Instant Withdrawals — Withdraw to ANY verified bank account in Nigeria (Access, GTBank, Zenith, First Bank, UBA, etc.). Must be in your name for security. Fast, safe, and reliable. Save bank accounts for quick future withdrawals.

6. Free Transfers Between HostFi Users — Send money to other HostFi users at zero cost. Use their email, phone number, or username. Instant delivery. No fees ever.

7. HXP (HostFi Points) Rewards Program — The HXP program is gradually wrapping up — earn while you can. Ways to earn HXP: deposit funds, swap crypto, log in daily, make trades, refer friends. Benefits: convert points to cash or crypto anytime. Rewards loyalty and activity.

8. Referral Program — Share your referral link with friends. When they join and start trading, both earn rewards. Everyone benefits.

9. Multi-Layer Security — 2FA via SMS, email, authenticator app, and PIN. Withdrawals only to verified accounts in your name. Every transaction logged and visible. Advanced encryption protocols. HostFi incorporates industry-leading security measures for accounts and transactions.

10. Customer Support — Available via live chat in app, email (help@hostfi.io), Telegram (@HostFiApp), and phone support. Active Telegram community for questions and updates.

Legal & Compliance
HostFi Services Ltd operates under the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria. HostFi maintains an Anti-Money Laundering & Combating the Financing of Terrorism (AML/CFT) Policy. KYC (Know Your Customer) and CDD (Customer Due Diligence) are required for all users. HostFi complies with the Money Laundering (Prohibition) Act, 2011 and Terrorism (Prevention) Act, 2011. Suspicious transactions are reported to the Nigerian Financial Intelligence Unit (NFIU). Users must be 13 years or older to use the service. Withdrawals are only allowed to bank accounts in the user's name for compliance and security purposes.

Privacy
HostFi collects personal data (email, name, phone, address) and usage data to provide and improve services. Data may be transferred to and processed in Nigeria. Users can request access, correction, or deletion of their personal data by contacting help@hostfi.io. HostFi uses cookies for session management, preferences, and security. Third-party analytics (Google Analytics) may be used.
